## What the user asked for, read against what exists
1. "Not manipulated by gravity fields, not pushed against trees or cave walls": today's top camera is the player as a rig. The player's `updateFrame` (player.cpp:278) turns its `up` toward the gravity sample under the rig, so in a flip room or on a planetoid the view flips with it; `ViewCamera::view` pulls the camera to `hit - 0.4` in front of any solid (view_camera.cpp), so under a canopy or in a cave the camera sits on the focus and sees nothing; `pan()` follows the ground with a ray from 40 m above the focus, so panning under trees climbs onto the crowns. Fly mode itself has no collision (player.cpp:293: `pos_ += mv`), so "pushed" is the camera pull-in, not the capsule.
2. Selection + gizmos: the editor has two corner keys (H / J), O copies the box to a clipboard file, P pastes with a rotation slider, no selection drawing, no move. Actions `stamp` and `paste` are logged with numbers; `paste` is refused in a networked session because the clipboard is a file of one machine.
3. Prefab instances: the registry (PrefabInfo) knows name, file, origin, rotation, size, slots, moving parts, manifest; parts and sockets are computed (`parts(id)`, `socket(id, name)`); it does *not* keep the variant and the parameters a placement used, there is no pick (which instance is at this cell), and placing an instance again at another spot already re-stamps it and rebuilds its nodes (PREFABS.md: "placing an instance again at another spot ... removes its old nodes before build"), which is the move operation in disguise.
4. Prefabs tab: only `door.prefab.lua` exists (demo level, Cove); the Cove's models folder has bench, crate, lantern_post, oak, birch, pine, bush, rock, tree_small as plain .vxm; no preview before placing. DebugLines draw boxes and arrows with a depth test (renderer.hpp:89); the renderer draws any `VoxelBody*` list it is given (`setBodies`, main.cpp:2110 `syncBodies`), and materials can be `transparent` (glass path, two layers).

## a) The god view and the player: a separate editor camera, with one logged action for streaming
Recommendation: **the editor camera does not touch the simulated player.** The player stays parked where it was (walking or flying as before, its state line unchanged, its plate still pressed, other peers see it standing there). The camera is machine-local view state like the third-person camera.

What the rig gave for free and how each comes back:
- Streaming and the level of detail follow the view: in the windowed, non-recorded game `streamer.update(chunkOfPos(fp.camera.position))` (main.cpp:4330) already follows the camera. In a recording or a lockstep session streaming is tick-driven around `simP0()` / `simPlayers[]`; add a logged action **`view_centre {cx, cz}`** sent when the editor's focus crosses a chunk (rate-limited to one per 10 ticks) and kept as `editorCentre[player]` in the sim; `tickTail` passes it to `streamer.setExtraCentres` (exists since 43ccab7) and to `freezeOutside`'s `others`. A replay and every peer then stream the same columns; bodies near the editor's view stay awake. Cost: a few bytes per chunk crossing. `lodEye` takes the camera.
- Recordings replay "the same view": they do not need to. A replay checks determinism; a replay viewer that wants the editor's view can get `camera.mode("top")` + `camera.focus` from a `--camera` setting later. Say in CAMERAS.md that the camera is not in the log.
- Gravity: the editor camera's `up` is world +y by default; a toggle `local_up` samples `gravity.fn()` at the focus (not at a rig) once per second for planetoids, eased. Nothing in the simulation reads it.
- Net: nobody's player flies into the sky; an editor in a game session is just a player standing still. (The rig behaviour is also a cheat in a game session: the rig's player cannot be hit.)
- Hand and seat: no conflict any more, the Home refusals from a1b0239 go.

What changes in the current implementation (this is what blocks the rest): remove the rig path in the frame loop (`if (topCam) player.setPose(...)`, `walkBeforeTop`, the `walking` / `mode` switching in the `camSeen` block, main.cpp:3993-4000 and 4155); `ViewCamera::view` for Top takes no eye and no pull-in ray (keep the pull-in for Third); `pan()`'s ground-follow becomes "the floor under the focus within +2 m / -40 m, scanning down from focus + 2 m" (so a crown 10 m up is never chosen) and refuses a climb over 2 m per second; keep `aim.cursor` / `aimEyeM()` / `aimDir()`, the free pointer, `camera.*` in Lua, Third mode. Rename for clarity: `CameraMode::Top` → `Editor` (keep "top" as an alias in `parseCameraMode`).

## b) The selection model
```cpp
struct Selection {                     // app state in EditorState (never in the sim, never in the hash)
    enum class Kind : u8 { None, Box, Prefab, Body } kind = Kind::None;
    IVec3 lo{0}, hi{-1};               // Box: inclusive voxel corners (also the bounds of a Prefab / Body for the handles)
    u32 id = 0;                        // Prefab: the instance id; Body: the body id
    std::shared_ptr<VoxelModel> drag;  // while a gizmo drags: the content being moved (a copy taken at drag start), for the ghost
    IVec3 dragOffset{0}; int dragRot = 0;
};
```
- **Box** is the primary kind. Drawing: LMB drag on the ground plane under the pointer picks the first corner (the aim ray's hit cell) and the opposite corner follows the pointer projected onto the plane of the first hit's top face; the height comes from Shift + wheel or the vertical face handle. H / J keep working as "set corner A / B at the look point".
- **Shown**: DebugLines outline (12 edges, width 2), eight corner handles (small boxes), six face handles (arrows at the face centres, the move gizmo), a rotation handle (a ring of 24 line segments around the vertical axis). A translucent fill is worth 40 lines in debugdraw.comp: a per-pixel ray/box test against up to 8 "fill boxes" blended at 20 %, depth-tested; it also previews the brush (cube / sphere bounds) and the paste box. Voxel-exact content previews are the ghost body of d).
- **Gizmo operations are actions with resolved numbers**, one journal group each (`sys.beginAction()`), undo = one step:
  - `region_fill {lo, hi, material, mode}` → `EditOp::box` (Delete = fill with air, Replace, FillAir, Paint).
  - `region_replace {lo, hi, from, to}` → a new `EditOp::replaceMaterial` (the one EditOp that does not exist yet; 30 lines in edit.cpp).
  - `clip_copy {idx, lo, hi}` → every peer copies the box from its own (identical) world into clip `idx`: **this makes the clipboard deterministic and lifts the "paste refused in a networked session" limit**, since `paste {idx, rot, origin}` then finds the same clip everywhere. The recording keeps writing the .vxm beside the log for the model editor, but a replay recomputes the clip from the world.
  - `region_move {lo, hi, dx, dy, dz}` → at apply time: copy the box, `clearRegion(lo, hi)`, `setModel(copy, lo + d)` in that order, one group. Resolved from the world on every peer: no model crosses the wire.
  - `region_rotate {lo, hi, rot}` (rot in 0..23, the 24 axis rotations `rotateModel` already does; the pivot is the box's minimum corner, the rotated box's origin is recomputed so the box's centre stays) and `region_mirror {lo, hi, axis}` (a `mirrorModel` helper, 15 lines). Free rotation and scaling are cut: voxels do not resample well; an integer `region_scale {k}` (nearest, ×2 / ÷2) can come later if asked.
  - `region_stamp_prefab {lo, hi, name}` = `define_prefab` from the selection: the MCP tool's code behind a button.
- **Drag preview without touching the world**: on mouse-down on a handle, `drag = copyRegion(world, lo, hi)`; while dragging, the ghost body (d) is placed at `lo + dragOffset` with `dragRot`; the world is untouched; mouse-up sends one action; Esc drops the drag. Snap: whole voxels; Shift constrains to the axis of the handle; Ctrl + drag = copy instead of move (the action is `paste` of a `clip_copy`).
- **Prefab** and **Body** kinds: a click on a voxel that a tracked prefab stamped selects the instance (c); a click on a body selects it (delete, freeze, copy to a model via `export_region` of its bounds). Multi-select and voxel-set selections (by material, connected flood) are phase 5.
- Keys in editor mode: LMB drag select / move a handle, RMB clear the selection, Delete, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V (clip_copy / paste with the ghost), R rotate the selection or the ghost by 90°, M mirror, F fill with the current material, Tab the panels as today. The Tools panel gets a "Selection" section with the numbers (lo / hi editable) and the same buttons, so everything is reachable without the mouse.

## c) Prefab instances in the world
Missing in prefab.*:
1. **Pick**: `u32 PrefabSystem::pick(const IVec3& cell) const` — the newest tracked instance whose box contains the cell and whose snapshot has a solid voxel there (or whose `partOf` names a part), so clicking the air inside a door frame selects the door. 20 lines; `partOf` and the snapshots are already there.
2. **The placement request kept**: PrefabInfo gains `variant` and `params` (the text form `prefabPlaceCode` already produces), saved in tracked.txt (bump kSaveVersion to 3: a v2 line has no variant / params, which reads as "defaults"; the writer writes v3). Without it a moved instance would come back with the manifest's defaults.
3. **Move / rotate / edit = place again**: `prefab_move {id, x, y, z, rotation}` and `prefab_set {id}` + text = the slots / variant / params in `prefabPlaceCode`'s form are both implemented as `prefab.place(name, ..., { name = instance, ... })` with the stored request, which re-stamps and rebuilds the nodes (the existing semantics). Two things to make sure of in prefab.cpp / lua_host.cpp: the old *children* of a nested prefab (`<instance>.<child>`) are removed before the parent's `build` places them again at the new spot (today only the nodes `<instance>.*` are removed; check `prefab.place` of an existing name with children), and a door's open angle is carried over or reset on purpose (reset is fine, say so).
4. **Handles**: the instance's box (PrefabInfo origin / size), its parts' boxes (`parts(id)`) and socket cells as small crosses, drawn with DebugLines when selected; the move gizmo is the box's face handles; R rotates by 90° about the vertical axis (rotation index arithmetic: the 24 rotations are indexed, a table `rotateAboutY[24]` gives the next index).
5. **Panel**: the Prefabs panel's manifest section already shows variant / slots / params for a *manifest*; the same widget with the *instance's* stored values and an "apply" button (= `prefab_set`), plus "move to the pointer", "rotate", "duplicate" (place again with a new instance name, offset by the size), "remove" (exists), "open the manifest" (log the path). Add `ToolBinding::selectedPrefab` (id) and `prefabSet` / `prefabMove` callbacks.
6. **Moving parts and bodies**: a door's panel body is re-created by `build` at the new spot (events.door removes the old node's body with the node). Loose bodies that happen to stand inside the instance's box are not moved (document). A region move that overlaps a tracked prefab's box should ask (or move the prefab with `prefab_move` and the rest with `region_move`); otherwise the registry's presence test marks the instance "missing" and a later re-stamp puts it back at the old spot. Rule for phase 2: a region move refuses (log line) while it overlaps a tracked instance; phase 3 lifts it.

## d) Starter prefabs and the ghost
**Ship as data, built by a script, mounted as a base pack.** `tools/make_prefabs.lua` (run by `voxelith --headless --lua tools/make_prefabs.lua --frames 1 --mcp`-style, or as a `--make-prefabs DIR` flag next to `--make-demo-assets`) builds each structure with `edit.*` on flat ground and calls the same code as the MCP `define_prefab` (export the box with parts and a manifest skeleton; the skeleton is then hand-edited once and committed). Output: `assets/prefabs/<name>.vxm` + `<name>.prefab.lua`, checked in, packaged as `assets/base.vxpack` (the pack system from 09cf20a overlays `models/` into every project; a project's own file of the same name wins). Starter kit (each a manifest with slots and 1–3 params; sizes in voxels):
- `lamp_post` (exists as a model; manifest adds the `lamp` node with `lit`, `color`), `torch` (emissive, `lit`), `bench` and `crate` (exist), `barrel`.
- `fence` (16 × 24 × 2, slots `post` / `rail`, variants `fence_4`, `fence_8` for longer runs), `gate` (a `door` manifest variant with `open = 90`, `dynamic = true`), `bridge_segment` (planks + rails, 32 long).
- `house_small` (96 × 64 × 96: walls, roof, a door socket that `build` fills with `ctx.place("door", ...)`, window slots `glass`, params `door = true`, `lamp = true`, `roof` material slot), `well`, `shed`.
- `oak`, `birch`, `pine`, `bush`, `rock` as plain models in the same pack (no manifest; the panel lists plain files already); `tree_small` with a `trunk` / `leaves` slot pair as the one manifest tree so slots are demonstrated on something organic.
`--check-project` already places every manifest once and runs `build`; make it also place the base pack's manifests, so the kit is tested by the project check.

**The ghost**: a render-only `VoxelBody` built from the rotated model (`buildModelBody(pool, mats, model, id)` with every voxel's material remapped to a new material `ghost`: `transparent = true`, cyan albedo, low alpha through the glass path), with an id in a reserved range (`0xF0000000 +`) so `renderer.registerBody` / `unregisterBody` work, appended to the `draw` list in `syncBodies` and never added to `PhysicsWorld` (so it is not in the hash, not a joint partner, not pushed, not an action). Moving it is `b->pos = origin * kVoxelM` per frame; rebuilding it on rotate is the cost of one `buildModelBody` (a 96^3 house is a few ms, acceptable on a key press; cap ghosts at 2 M voxels and fall back to the outline box for bigger selections). The same ghost serves the paste preview, the prefab-placement preview from the panel (the model with the slots applied, following the pointer's ground hit, R rotates, click places, Esc cancels) and the move drag. The debug-overlay route (lines only) is the fallback, a GPU model draw path is not needed.

Cut: free rotation, non-integer scale, a retained gizmo framework, selection shared between peers, undo of selection changes (selection is UI state), a GPU ghost path.

Risks: (1) `region_move` across chunk borders not yet streamed in: the EditOp path defers edits to unloaded chunks (`deferred_`): fine, but the copy at apply time reads an unloaded chunk as air: refuse a move whose box touches an unloaded chunk (`world.isLoaded`). (2) The ghost's bricks come from the brick pool: a 2 M-voxel ghost is ~4k bricks, fine; free it on deselect. (3) `view_centre` spam while panning fast: the rate limit; and a peer who never enters editor mode pays the extra streaming of the editor's area (that is the point, say so). (4) The `transparent` material path draws two layers: a ghost over glass over water shows one layer less; cosmetic. (5) Prefab re-placement resets node state (door angle, counters); acceptable for an editor move, documented. (6) tracked.txt v3: the SAVES.md rule (reader for v2 kept, refusal of newer) applies.

Total: about 4.5 days of the builder's time; phases 0 and 1 give the user the god view and copy / fill / paste in the first day, which is most of the request.

## Array (2026-10-21)
Select a box or a placed prefab, tick **array** in the Tools panel: the outline of every copy is drawn (and, for a box, the content as a translucent ghost at the last copy). Copies (1..64, **Alt + wheel** changes it), a direction (+x ... -z) and a gap in voxels (0: end to end, negative: overlapping); **Enter** or "make the array" does it. Actions `region_array {lo, hi, dx, dy, dz, count}` (the box is read once, the copies are set in order, air included; refused when a copy would reach unloaded chunks or touch a placed prefab) and `prefab_array {id, dx, dy, dz, count}` (each copy is a `prefab_move` copy, so an instance of its own with its nodes). Both are one undo step: `DestructionSystem::holdGroup` keeps the placements of one action in one journal group. MCP `region op:"array"` and `prefab_array`. Not built: a two-axis grid, a rotated array (a staircase, a ring), array as a Lua call.

## Gravity zones (2026-10-21)
Everything that can be said in Lua `gravity.add` is one logged action now: `gravity_define {id, create}` with the field as JSON text (`app/field_json.*`: `fieldToJson` / `fieldFromJson`, validated: finite numbers in range, at most 64 spline points, all or nothing), MCP `gravity_add / gravity_set / gravity_remove / gravity_enable / gravity_sample` (docs/MCP.md has the recipes of the habitat tube, the flip room and the twist tube; `tests/test_field_json.cpp` rebuilds them from text and compares the gravity at 400 points). The older `gravity_set` / `gravity_add` actions (a fixed list of numbers, no line ends or spline points) are still read from old logs.
In the editor (Gravity window): **zone from the selection**: draw a box with the selection tool, choose box (one direction: up, down, an axis), sphere (toward / away from the centre), tube (a line along the box's longest axis, toward / away from it; "away" is the habitat tube you walk on the inside of) or weightless box, strength, the fade beyond the shape, priority and name, then "create the zone" (`app/gravity_zone.*`: `zoneFromBox`). Every field with a box form has **select its box** (the selection becomes the zone's bounds: the handles resize it, the move handle moves it) and **fit to the selection** (`zoneRefit` writes the box back and keeps the other settings). "gravity field shells" in the View window draws the fields. Not built: handles drawn on the field itself, a direction arrow, planes, splines and surfaces (MCP only), a zone as a prefab part.
