Findings worth keeping:
- **Averaging equal-priority fields dilutes gravity** where two planets' shells overlap; additive fields on a null baseline behave like real bodies and keep the surface pull intact. The saddle between two planets is simply where the pulls cancel; the head of a standing capsule hits the other planet if the gap is under ~4 m.
- Head-first launches are fine: the up vector flips on the way (rate scaled by field strength) and the player lands feet-first.
- Walking on a Surface field climbs *any* solid, including the ruin's wall: that is the intended "walk on any destructible shape"; the solid radius option keeps the ruin's upper floors out of planet A's grid.
- The Surface grid caps distances at the field's `outer`, and smoothing (4 m) is larger than a 2 m crater: gravity in a crater points mostly at the planet centre (rubble rolls down into the bowl), use a smaller smoothing to see walls pulling.
- Analytic fields use float, not the fixed point the design mentions (same machine determinism only).

Known gaps / next:
- GPU baked volume is a debug consumer only (no particles until Phase 4); regions are re-uploaded whole when the revision moves.
- No SIMD batch sampler (scalar loop, parallel over jobs), no incremental Surface rebuild window (full rebuild ~10 ms), spline nearest point is brute force over ~24 samples per span.
- Ragdoll is a prop (no active animation / character ragdoll hookup, no joint motors, no self-collision between non-adjacent limbs is disabled only for jointed pairs).
- Rubble thrown out of the shells floats in the weightless box until it leaves it (then falls to the ground field).
- Phase 2 open points were worked off in the Phase 4 session, see the section "Phase 2 open points closed" below.

- **Vapor** (`sim/vapor_system.*`): water that boils away (lava meets water: the solver reports `Evaporation` events per brick in a deterministic order; a burning voxel next to water drains 14 fill) becomes steam parcels (CPU point masses: amount in fill units, rise against the local up at about 2.5 m/s with a slow wander, slide along ceilings, merge per 12 voxel cell, cap 4,000). They cool (0.35 %/tick in the open, 4 %/tick pressed against a ceiling) and what cools falls as **Drop** particles (24 fill each; 90 % of the loss under a ceiling, 40 % in the open, the rest "mixes into the air"; test: condensed + dispersed + in the air + pending == evaporated exactly). Visuals: a trickle of Puff particles per parcel. **Drop** (new GPU class): falls, stops on the first thing it touches incl. water (`gSkipT` mode 0), reports landings in liquid (adds fill through `LiquidSystem::addFill`), on Ember (puts the fire out, `FireSystem::extinguish`) and on lava (hiss: more steam); ordinary ground hits are not reported. **Rain**: Y (or script `rain:1`) rains 40 drops/tick over a 17 m disc above the player. HUD line "vapor ...". The liquid yard has a gap low in the lava trough wall: breach the tank (`dig:16`) and the flood meets the seeping lava (steam, stone).

The liquid yard no longer floats over the tower spawn (it moved 440 voxels west and stands on a solid block down to the lowest ground).

## Non-obvious findings (keep!)
- **FIFO/vsync presents stall exactly 1000 ms** on the virtual :90 display (no real vblank). The app uses IMMEDIATE present plus its own 60 fps limiter (`--fps N`, `--vsync` to opt in).
- **Do not `XCloseDisplay` at exit**: NVIDIA's Vulkan driver hooks libX11's close chain and the code is unloaded after `vkDestroyInstance` -> segfault at exit.
- vcpkg's volk lib has no Xlib entry points: `volk.c` from the vcpkg include dir is compiled into `vx_render` instead of linking `volk::volk`.
- The X screen is 1920x1080 (service `ExecStartPre` runs xrandr). Window = whole screen; internal render resolution is capped at 1920 wide and blitted.
- `-Wno-missing-field-initializers` is project-wide on purpose (Vulkan `Info{sType}` idiom).
- Sanitizer build: `cmake --preset release -B build/asan -DVCPKG_INSTALLED_DIR=$PWD/build/release/vcpkg_installed -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=RelWithDebInfo "-DCMAKE_CXX_FLAGS=-stdlib=libc++ -fsanitize=address,undefined -fno-omit-frame-pointer" "-DCMAKE_EXE_LINKER_FLAGS=-fsanitize=address,undefined <toolchain ld flags>"` (lldb/gdb are unusable here: lldb lacks libpython).
- No validation layer is installed (`VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation` missing); `--validate` only warns. Possible to fetch the layer into ~/tools later.

## Deviations from / open points against the design doc
- GPU world uses a **dense toroidal chunk directory** per level (window around the camera) instead of a hash.
- Bindless arrays are not used yet; one descriptor set with fixed bindings (pool, chunks, dir, materials, UBO, G-buffer images, HUD buffer).
- Far LOD is generated procedurally; edited fine chunks re-derive their level-1/2 cells through `LodUpdater` (Phase 2). Coarse levels drop windows/doors so far towers read as solid blocks. No dither/blend at LOD seams yet.
- Chunk-level and brick-level temporal reuse and the 8x8 tile pre-pass are not done; bevelled normals not done; water/glass are opaque.
- Per-chunk undo journal and unloaded-chunk edit journal not done (Phase 2).
- Edit application is synchronous in the main loop: a big noisy carve costs a few ms (hitch risk); move to a job / double-buffer in Phase 2.
- Upload hazard: a freed brick slot reused by another chunk can show stale data for a frame or more if the upload budget defers the first chunk. Documented, not fixed.
