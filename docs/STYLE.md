# Style of the imported kits, furniture, props and characters

The artist's reference for what the existing models look like and how to make a new one that sits next to them. Same method as docs/TREE_STYLE.md: **measured numbers from the files** (a script read every voxel), a recipe, and what does *not* fit. Anything that is not measured says *(guess)*. The trees have their own document (docs/TREE_STYLE.md); this one is the buildings, rooms, furniture, props and figures.

**The script is `tools/style_stats.py`** (no dependencies, 13 s for all 160 models): `tools/style_stats.py --families` writes every table below from the files; `tools/style_stats.py model.vxm` measures one file; **`tools/style_stats.py --compare furniture new.vxm`** prints the numbers of a new model next to the range of its family and marks what lies outside it (exit code 1 if something is more than 10 % of the range outside). `--write-reference` regenerates `tools/style_reference.json` (the ranges `--compare` uses) after the families change. Colours come from the materials tables (built-in, `assets/prefabs/materials.csv`, the dungeon kit's and the game's `materials.csv`, or any `--materials FILE.csv`) and from **paint** (docs/PAINT.md) where a voxel is painted. Pictures of every family (one model each, 2 to 4 m away): `~/share/style/`.

## Read this first: what the files are, and what was lost on the way
- **The `.vox` / mesh sources are not in the repository.** What was measured are the `.vxm` models the engine uses (the converted art, `assets/prefabs`, the dungeon kit in `projects/dungeon_kit`, the game's `models/`). Numbers about the original artists' work are therefore numbers about the **converted** models.
- **The conversion changed colours.** `voxelith --convert-vox` matches a colour to an existing material, so many pack colours became the engine's own `wood` (#8c6238), `stone` (#808084), `dirt` (#6e4e34), `char`, `metal`, `snow`: 21 % of all the voxels of the 155 models (31 % of a model on average) are builtin materials, which is why the "most used" colours of three different packs are the same brown. A new model should use the **pack's own** `xx-RRGGBB` materials (or paint) for what has to match a pack, and the builtin wood / stone only where the existing models do.
- **Two voxel sizes are mixed**: models stored at **3.75 cm** (the Characters, Furniture and Voxel Magic pub packs: a one-part rig with scale 0.6) and at **6.25 cm** (the world's voxel: dungeon kit, desert houses, graveyard, the engine's generated kit, the creatures and the game's props). Stamped into the world (`prefab.place`) the fine ones are resampled to 6.25 cm; drawn as bodies or models (`model.spawn{ visual = true }`, `player.model`, `agent.spawn{ collision = "proxy" }`) they keep their detail. A new piece must say which size it is at.
- The engine's **built-in surface patterns and bump** (docs/PAINT.md, docs/BUMP.md) now draw brick, planks, tiles and stone on every model whose material has one, so a picture of a model shows colour *plus* a pattern and recessed seams: the colour tables below are the voxel colours, not the picture.

## The recipe in ten lines
1. A model is **one connected piece** (median 1; furniture 1 to 13 loose bits like chair legs) built of **flat colour regions**, not of a gradient and not stippled like the trees.
2. **Architecture** is a wall / floor / pillar / door **module of 41 voxels (2.56 m)**, 2 to 3 voxels thick for walls, with a **base shade that covers 55 to 75 %** of the surface and four or five other shades of the same warm hue scattered over it (mortar-like, no outline).
3. **Colour**: one hue family per piece; a ramp has **2 to 3 shades** in furniture, props and figures (**5 to 8** in the dungeon kit's masonry), value ratio darkest : lightest about 1.4 to 2.1 (figures 2.3 to 14 with their black eyes and white teeth).
4. **No outline, no highlight rim, no shading in the voxel colours.** Edge voxels are as bright as face voxels (median ratio 0.92 to 1.0; the dungeon kit 0.89 to 1.01); the darkest colour is on the edges 0 to 2 % of the time (characters). The engine lights it.
5. **Silhouettes are simple boxes and cylinders** with a few steps: a wall is a 41 x 41 x 2 box (the bounding box's 12 edges are 100 % solid), a pillar or a desert wall has its corners stepped (15 % of the edge voxels).
6. **Furniture is small**: a chair 0.53 x 1.01 x 0.53 m, a table 1.31 x 0.49 x 0.53 m (low), a bed 0.56 x 0.71 x 1.09 m, a wardrobe 1.24 x 1.28 x 0.71 m at 3.75 cm voxels: about 0.6 of real size (the pack was drawn beside the figures).
7. **Figures are chibi-ish**: a head is 16 to 26 % of the height (1/6 to 1/4), arms and legs 4 to 7 voxels thick at 3.75 cm (15 to 26 cm; the creatures' 1 to 2 voxels at 6.25 cm), the body in **flat regions of 2 to 3 shades per part** (clumping 2 to 5), T-pose in the pack, arms at the sides after rigging.
8. **Characters are symmetric** left to right (shape match 0.97 median), props mostly (0.98), architecture 0.97.
9. **Scale to the doors**: a door is 1.06 x 2.06 m, a human 1.8 to 2.0 m, a monster 1.8 to 2.1 m, a golem 3.2 m.
10. **Check it**: `tools/style_stats.py --compare <family> new.vxm`.

## The measures
| measure | what it is |
|---|---|
| `voxels`, `bbox`, `metres`, `voxel_cm` | solid voxels, bounding box in voxels, in metres (the file's voxel size times its rig scale), the voxel's edge in cm |
| `bbox_fill` | solid share of the bounding box |
| `colours`, `ramps`, `shades_per_ramp`, `value_ratio` | distinct colours; groups of one hue (within 28 degrees; greys one group); shades in a ramp (ramps of at least 5 % of the voxels); lightest / darkest value of the ramp with most shades |
| `dark`, `light`, `saturation` | lowest and highest luma, mean saturation weighted by voxels |
| `clumping` | how often two neighbouring surface voxels have the same colour, divided by what independent random colours with the same shares would give. **1.0 = drawn at random per voxel like the trees; 2 and more = regions** |
| `value_vs_height` | correlation of brightness with height (a gradient shows as far from 0) |
| `edge_share`, `edge_vs_face_value`, `darkest_on_edges` | share of surface voxels that are corners or ridges; their brightness against flat faces (below 0.85 an outline, above 1.15 a highlight); how often the darkest colour is on them |
| `bbox_edge_fill` | solid share along the 12 edges of the bounding box (1.0 a sharp box, small = stepped or rounded) |
| `mirror_x_shape`, `mirror_x_colour` | left-right symmetry of shape and of colour |
| `pieces`, `largest_piece` | 6-connected pieces and the share of the biggest |
| `head_height_share`, `head_width_share` | a rigged figure's `head` part against the whole (the figures have named parts; the creatures too) |

## Architecture (46 models: dungeon kit, desert houses, the pub bar, the engine's walls, arches and ruins)
**Sizes.** The dungeon kit is a **41-voxel module (2.56 m)**: wall `WallBrick_Tall` 2.56 x 2.56 x 0.12 m (3,362 voxels), door frame 2.56 x 2.56 x 0.12, floor corner 2.56 x 0.56 x 2.56, pillar 0.56 x 2.56 x 0.56 m, door 1.06 x 2.06 x 0.19. The desert houses are 3 to 4.3 m wide and 4.8 to 12.5 m tall (small building 3 to 3.5 x 4.8 to 7.5 x 4.1 to 4.3 m, tall building 3 to 3.5 x 10 to 12.5 x 4.3 to 4.8 m, wall 1.5 x 5.4 x 0.25 m, arch 4.3 x 4 x 1.75 m, large building 11 x 5.1 x 5.8 m: 234,000 voxels). The engine's own kit: wall 4 x 1.75 x 0.5 m, house 6 x 5.6 x 6 m, hut 3 x 3.1 x 3 m, stairs 2 x 1 x 3 m, fence 4 x 1.3 x 0.19 m.
**Palette.** Dungeon kit: `dk-a3845c` (163,132,92) is the base, with `dk-917052`, `dk-80614b`, `dk-ad9168`, `dk-735849` (hue 24 to 36, saturation .3 to .4, value .45 to .68): a wall has 5 shades (57 / 14 / 14 / 11 / 4 %), the floor 7 (74 % `dk-8e7d67`), the pillar 6 (60 % base). Desert: `bd-877553` (135,117,83) 77 % with `bd-615442` as the dark (the walls: **2 colours**, 77 / 23 %), buildings 7 to 10 colours of sand, brown (`bd-48332b`, `bd-35241d`) and terracotta (`bd-a85c3e`, `bd-825a4f`), trims; pub bar 33 colours. The engine's kit: 1 to 4 builtin materials (`stone`, `wood`, `brick`, `concrete`, `dirt`).
**Shading.** Dungeon kit `clumping` 1.4 to 1.9: a dominant base with random single voxels of the other shades (value ratio 1.5 to 1.9), no gradient (`value_vs_height` about 0). Desert houses 2.2 to 3.6: patches (plaster and brick areas, a few darker blocks drawn into the walls, as in the picture `~/share/style/architecture_desert_small_building_1_x2.png`). Engine kit: 1.0 (a single colour; the engine's pattern is the texture).
**Edges.** No outline: `edge_vs_face_value` median 1.0 (dungeon kit 0.89 to 1.01, desert 0.73 to 0.84 = slightly darker trims, not a drawn line). Walls and the door frame are sharp boxes (`bbox_edge_fill` 1.0 / 0.79 for the floor); pillars and the desert walls are **stepped** (0.15): *(guess)* a pillar is a stack of slightly different squares.
**Assembly.** One piece (median 1). The desert houses have loose bits (awnings, window frames: 25 to 28 pieces in small buildings 2 and 4, 1 to 3 in the others). Modules join on the 41-voxel grid.
**Does not fit** (measured or seen): `desert_street_lamp_x2` and `_x3` are stored **lying along z** (6 x 10 x 100 voxels: a 6.25 m lamp post turned on its side; rotate it before use); the engine's generated kit (`structures/*`, `stone_arch`, `bridge_segment`, `path_tile`, `stairs`, `pillar`, `wall_corner`, `ruins/*`) is **one colour, one material** (clumping 1.0) with no hand-placed variation, unlike every pack; `pub_bar` (33 colours, 3.75 cm voxels, `mg-`) is another style (below); `graveyard_ground` is 139,000 voxels of cold grey and `graveyard_*` is cold and desaturated (hue 203 to 229) against the dungeon kit's warm browns.

## Furniture (26 models: the furniture and kitchen packs, the pub set, the game's kit)
**Sizes** (3.75 cm voxels): bed 0.56 x 0.71 x 1.09 m (15 x 19 x 29 voxels), chair 0.53 x 1.01 x 0.53, table 1.31 x 0.49 x 0.53, couch 1.2 x 0.79 x 0.68, drawer 0.79 x 0.71 x 0.53, wardrobe 1.24 x 1.28 x 0.71 (18,000 voxels), oven 0.9 m cube, pub table 0.68 x 0.94 x 1.58, pub stool 0.53 x 0.64 x 0.53, mug 0.26 m, bottles 0.56 x 0.53 x 0.19; median 841 voxels, range 59 to 18,165. The game's own `props` (`table`, `chair`, `bench`, at 6.25 cm) are **2.5 x 1.38 x 1.5 m, 0.88 x 1.94 x 0.88 m and 1.5 x 0.62 x 0.5 m**: 1.9 to 4.7 times the size of the pack's.
**Palette.** 1 to 8 colours (median 4), ramps 1 to 4, **2 shades per ramp** (median; 1 to 5), value ratio 1.3 to 2.2 (median 1.6), lightest colour median .61, darkest .24. Woods are builtin `wood` (#8c6238) or `tr-563f2e` / `tr-43341b`; the pack adds pure saturated colours: dark green (#002200 / #001100 table and chair variants), cyan (#99cccc), blue (#0066cc); pub set: greys (#4b4b4b), cream (#fde4e4), oranges (#ffca7b, #db8f53, #d09416).
**Shading.** Regions: `clumping` median 1.42 (1.07 to 3.3), the same wood on a whole leg or top, with 1 to 2 extra shades; no gradient. The pub pieces are the most contrasty (table 3.3).
**Edges.** No outline in the furniture pack (median 0.92); the **pub set's edges are darker than its faces** (stool 0.43, table 0.52, `pub_bottles` 1.07): hand-darkened rims or a dark inner colour (guess: a dark outline from the pub's own artist).
**Silhouette.** Boxes with thin legs (the table: a thin slab on four thin legs; the chair: a high rounded back with a pattern of lighter slats; see the pictures in `~/share/style/`). **Symmetric**: `mirror_x_shape` 1.0 for the median.
**Assembly.** One piece, or loose bits (chair 13 pieces, table 4 at 6-connectivity; *(guess)* legs that touch the seat only along an edge or a corner).
**Does not fit**: the game's `table`, `chair`, `bench` (6.25 cm, one flat colour, 2 to 4 times bigger); the pub set (dark edges, 29 colours); the oven (0.9 m cube, 5 to 6 colours, edge 0.65 to 0.7: darker rims).

## Mixed packs: what does not sit together (summary)
1. **3.75 cm vs 6.25 cm voxels**: pack furniture, figures and the pub (fine) next to the dungeon kit, desert, props, graveyard (coarse): details of 2 voxels are 7.5 cm in one and 12.5 cm in the other.
2. **Muted warm masonry (dungeon kit, desert) vs saturated pure colours (furniture pack, creatures, characters)**.
3. **Cold grey graveyard** (hue 203 to 229, saturation .12) beside everything warm.
4. **Single-colour engine kit** pieces (`structures`, `ruins`, `buildings/*.vxm`, game `props/nature`) next to hand-shaded packs.
5. **Dark-edged pub set** (`mg-`) next to the no-outline rest.
6. **The game's furniture** (table, chair, bench: 1.9 to 4.7 times the pack's size).
7. **`desert_street_lamp_*` lying on its side.**
8. **Brightly rimmed banners and small chests** (rim 1.4 to 2.3 times brighter than the face).

## Rules for a new piece
1. **Decide the family and the voxel size** (3.75 cm for anything beside the pack figures and furniture, 6.25 cm for architecture, props and creatures); a one-part rig with scale 0.6 stores the fine ones (`voxelith --convert-vox IN.vox OUT.vxm --vox-unit 0.6`, docs/ANIMATION.md "Voxel scale").
2. **Architecture**: build on the **41-voxel module**; walls 2 to 3 voxels thick, doors 17 x 33 voxels, pillars 9 x 41; one connected piece; edges sharp on walls and frames, stepped on pillars and trims.
3. **One hue family per piece** from the pack's own colours: dungeon kit `dk-a3845c`, `dk-917052`, `dk-80614b`, `dk-ad9168`, `dk-735849` (stone), `dk-8e7d67` (floor); desert `bd-877553`, `bd-615442`, `bd-48332b`, `bd-a85c3e`; or paint. **Base shade 55 to 75 % of the surface**, the others scattered: furniture, props and figures 2 to 3 shades per colour (value ratio 1.4 to 2.1), masonry 5 to 8.
4. **No outline, no highlight rim, no baked shading**: edge voxels as bright as faces (ratio 0.9 to 1.05). The one exception in the pack is cloth borders (banners).
5. **Flat regions, not noise**: clumping 1.4 to 2 for furniture and props, 2 to 5 for figures; 1.0 only for stippled foliage.
6. **Furniture** at about 0.6 real size: chair 0.53 x 1.0 x 0.53 m, table 1.3 x 0.5 x 0.5 (a coffee-table height) at 3.75 cm voxels; **figures** 1.8 to 2.0 m (monsters 1.8 to 2.1, a boss up to 3.2 m), head 0.16 to 0.26 of the height, limbs **4 to 7 voxels at 3.75 cm, solid (the pack figures' family: decided 2026-10-24, the game's creatures move to it; the `make_creatures.py` line-limbed creatures of 1 to 2 voxels at 6.25 cm are the old style)**, 9 to 13 colours, symmetric, facing +z, standing on y = 0, a rig with the part names of the table above.
7. **Materials**: register every colour as `xx-RRGGBB` with `tools/vox_materials.py` (classify: wood / stone / metal / cloth / glass) and keep the count to the table above (1 to 10 for a prop, 9 to 13 for a figure, 5 to 8 for a stone module).
8. **Surface**: give a masonry, wood or tile material the engine's pattern and a `bump` (docs/BUMP.md: brick 110, planks 70, stone 60, tiles 40 to 50) instead of drawing seams into the voxels; a model that draws its own seams (the dungeon wall's bricks are voxel colours) needs `bump` 0 or a pattern-less material.
9. **Check**: `tools/style_stats.py --compare architecture|furniture|props|characters new.vxm`, then a picture 2 to 4 m away beside the pack (`~/share/style/` has the reference one for each family).
